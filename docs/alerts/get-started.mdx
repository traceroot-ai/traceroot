---
title: Get started with alerts
description: Monitor project metrics and notify your team when a threshold is crossed
---

Threshold alerts monitor an aggregate over a time window, such as latency or error rate, and notify your team when it meets the rule's condition. They are configured from the project's **Alerts** page. Detector findings have a separate [notification workflow](/detectors/alerts).

## Configure notifications

Threshold alerts post to the Slack channel connected to the workspace. Ask a workspace ADMIN to connect Slack and select a destination channel in workspace settings. The Alerts page links to these settings when a connection or channel is missing. Slack availability depends on your plan.

## Create an alert

1. Open **Alerts** in your project's navigation and start a new alert.
2. Choose a metric and aggregation, then add filters to select the traffic you want to monitor.
3. Set the comparison, threshold, and lookback window. Inspect the preview to check the rule against recent data.
4. Choose whether to notify only on transitions or to re-alert at a regular interval while the condition remains breached.
5. Give the alert a name and click **Create Alert**.

Creating, editing, pausing, and resuming alerts requires MEMBER or ADMIN access. VIEWERs can read alerts. See [workspace roles](/getting-started/concepts#roles).

## Review and maintain alerts

The Alerts list shows each rule's state, severity, and window. Open a rule to edit its metric, filters, condition, or notification settings. Use the actions menu to pause a rule when you want to stop monitoring temporarily, then resume it when needed.

If notifications are missing, check the workspace's Slack connection and selected channel as well as the rule's state. Check the time window and filters if the preview contains no matching data.
